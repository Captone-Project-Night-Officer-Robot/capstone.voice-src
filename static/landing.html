01  —  ABOUT

Autonomous Security
for the Modern Era

The Night Officer is an autonomous night-patrol welfare robot. Built on a Yahboom four-wheel-drive chassis with a Raspberry Pi 4 brain, it patrols indoor spaces, watches for people who have fallen, and stays with them until human help arrives.

When its YOLO26n vision model confirms a fall, the robot stops, drops a pin on the live map, and a conversational AI agent speaks with the person in English and Korean — calmly gathering their name, injuries, and condition.

It then dispatches help automatically: an outbound emergency call and SMS relay the patient's details to the medical team. The whole patrol streams live to a real-time mission-control dashboard — all inside a custom 3D-printed shell designed and fabricated by our team.

02  —  CAPABILITIES

What The Night Officer Can Do

👁
FALL DETECTION

A YOLO26n vision model running on a remote inference server spots when a person has fallen, with a verification window to reject false alarms before acting.

YOLO26n · REAL-TIME
🗣
AI VOICE COMPANION

On a confirmed fall, a conversational agent speaks with the person in English and Korean — gathering their name, injuries, and condition while keeping them calm.

LIVEKIT · ELEVENLABS · GROQ
📞
EMERGENCY 119 DISPATCH

The robot places an autonomous outbound phone call and SMS, relaying the patient's details and location to the medical team — no human in the loop.

TWILIO · SIP · SMS
🧭
AUTONOMOUS NAVIGATION

White-line tracking with PID steering keeps the robot on its patrol route, while dual ultrasonic sensors stop and steer it around obstacles.

LINE-FOLLOW · ULTRASONIC ×2

LIVE MISSION CONTROL
